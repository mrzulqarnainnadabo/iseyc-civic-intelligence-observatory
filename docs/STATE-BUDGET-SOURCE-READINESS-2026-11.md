# Nigeria State Budget & Planning Source Readiness

**Review date:** 12 November 2026  
**Status:** Source discovery and partial secondary analysis; not a complete 36-state budget dataset.

## Executive finding

The Observatory can now present a dated, source-attributed view of national fiscal context and selected state budget performance. It cannot yet claim complete, primary-source budget coverage for all 36 states and the FCT.

- **15 of 36 state official budget portals** were located in this source-discovery pass. Their documents still require extraction and line-item validation.
- **18 states remain unreviewed** in this pass. This means discovery is incomplete, not that their documents are unavailable.
- The Nigeria Governors' Forum Public Finance Database describes comparable original, revised and actual fiscal data for all 36 states. Its landing page rendered unrelated injected third-party links during this review, so the portal is under integrity review and no figures have been ingested from it.
- Agora Policy's 2025 state-budget review covers **35 of 36 states**, excluding Rivers.
- BudgIT's H1 2026 revenue review covers **34 of 36 states**, excluding Rivers and Osun.
- The FCT is tracked separately. A secondary report of its 2026 budget passage was found, but the final enacted FCT appropriation law and official implementation reports were not confirmed.

The Observatory must show these denominators beside every comparison. It must never turn partial coverage into a national claim.

## What the current evidence says

### Q2 2026 fiscal information availability — all 36 states

BudgIT's States Fiscal Transparency League provides a Q2 2026 score for **all 36 states**. Its components are citizens' budgets (15 points), quarterly Budget Implementation Reports (16), e-procurement portals (11) and state fiscal-data websites (12), normalised to a total score out of 100.

- Eight states score 100: Adamawa, Benue, Ebonyi, Ekiti, Gombe, Jigawa, Kaduna and Kebbi.
- Sixteen states score 90 or above.
- Rivers scores 15, the lowest in the table; Nasarawa scores 57 and Osun 67.
- Kano and Niger score zero for e-procurement; Rivers also scores zero.
- Osun and Rivers score zero for quarterly BIR publication; Nasarawa and Rivers score zero for citizens' budget availability.

**Source:** [BudgIT States Fiscal Transparency League — Q2 2026](https://fiscalleague.budgit.org/latest-ranking)

This is a measure of fiscal information availability and completeness, not a measure of public-service delivery, government effectiveness, public trust or corruption. The Observatory preserves the four component scores as well as the total so users can see what drives the score.

## Required evidence pack for each state

For every state and the FCT, collect the following in order:

1. **Planning:** MTEF/Fiscal Strategy Paper, Medium-Term Sector Strategies, approved sector plans and citizens' budget.
2. **Authority:** original approved budget, appropriation law, supplementary/revised budget and virement records.
3. **Execution:** Q1–Q4 Budget Implementation Reports with original and revised budget, releases, actual expenditure, commitments and balances.
4. **Audit:** Accountant-General financial statements, Auditor-General report, audited accounts and audit queries.
5. **Procurement:** procurement plan, tender/award, contract amount, contractor, payment certificates, variations and completion status.
6. **Delivery:** named project location, coordinates, completion evidence, commissioning, functionality and independent field verification.
7. **Sector outcomes:** education, health, water, agriculture, transport, digital infrastructure and other relevant service indicators with their own reference periods.

## How each department should interpret the fiscal evidence

| Department | Questions it should answer | Evidence that must accompany a conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Education & Capacity Building | What is budgeted for basic/secondary education, teachers, classrooms, learning materials and skills? What was actually spent? | MDA and programme allocations, UBEC counterpart funding, BIR outturn, school census reporting rate, learning assessment and school-level verification |
| Health & Well-being | What is budgeted for PHC, hospitals, workforce, essential medicines, equipment and health insurance? What reached facilities? | State health BIR, BHCPF/DAI records, staffing, NHFR facility identity, NHLMIS stock data, functionality and field verification |
| Software & Technology | What is budgeted for ICT infrastructure, connectivity, digital skills, innovation and public digital services? What is operational? | Procurement and contract data, network coverage, programme completion, active users, uptime, digital-skills outcomes and independent validation |
| Civic Department | Are budgets, procurement, audits and implementation records public? Which institution has the documented duty? | Appropriation law, fiscal reports, audit findings, procurement records, official mandates and published response records |

The Observatory supplies evidence and uncertainty. The departments interpret their respective fields and propose work; the Digital Operations Centre assigns owners, deadlines, review and follow-up. No department should infer local service conditions from a national or state budget line alone.

## Publication and comparison rules

- Original budget, revised budget, releases, cash expenditure and physical delivery are separate fields.
- Keep both the original and revised budget. Never silently replace one with the other.
- Preserve the reporting period, publication date, source URL, unit, geography, denominator, extraction method and revision history.
- Reconcile fiscal years, accounting bases, chart-of-account classifications and reporting cut-off dates before comparing states.
- A blank or unavailable report is not zero.
- Do not rank states where reporting coverage or definitions are not comparable.
- Secondary analysis may guide discovery and cross-checks, but the original state BIR and audited accounts remain the preferred source.
- No AI-generated estimate may fill a missing state value. Human review is required before national presentation.

## Current implementation boundary

The Observatory source-snapshot API is `GET /api/evidence/source-snapshots`. It is a curated public transcription endpoint, not a live connection to every state portal. The existing `GET /api/evidence/snapshots` Evidence Gateway contract is kept unchanged for current consumers.

The 36-state coverage register and the source catalogue expose what is located, partial, under review or not yet reviewed. Numeric state-level observations will be added only after source documents have been extracted and validated.
