# Geographic reference v1

## Hierarchy

Nigeria → State/FCT → LGA → Ward → Community

## v1 scope

- All 36 states + FCT names in `states-ng.v1.json`
- Stable product identifiers preferred over free text where possible
- LGA/ward **not** fully enumerated in v1 — do not invent incomplete lists

## Coverage honesty

- Presence on a national map ≠ research coverage ≠ collected evidence ≠ verified findings
- Pilot published Mandate records must not be presented as nationally representative

## Source attribution

Document dataset version, retrieval date, and licence notes. Prefer official gazette / NPC / state gazette for future LGA packs.
